# Conflict of interest policy v0.1

**Status:** Current Draft — subject to legal and institutional review.  
**Related:** [`funding-and-financial-integrity.md`](../../02-policies/institutional/funding-and-financial-integrity.md)  
**Counsel review:** Not started

## Purpose

Prevent self-dealing, related-party capture, and biased funding or distribution decisions.

## Rules

1. Disclose material conflicts before voting on or approving funding, grants, sponsorships, or distributions.
2. Recuse from decisions where a personal financial interest is material.
3. Related-party transactions require enhanced / reserved approval.
4. Keep a conflict register for officers, domain stewards, and distribution approvers.

## Prohibitions

- Approving one’s own contributor payout without independent review.
- Steering restricted funds to entities controlled by the decision-maker without disclosure and enhanced approval.
- Hiding investor or sponsor relationships that affect neutrality.

## Open questions

- Disclosure form and retention period
- Who maintains the conflict register
- Materiality thresholds
