**Status:** Current Draft — subject to legal and institutional review. Not authorization to accept grant funds.

## Purpose

Define how Civizen may accept and administer grants and restricted public-interest funds without creating investor rights or surrendering mission independence.

The legal entity or entities capable of receiving grants will be determined through Civizen’s institutional and legal architecture. Until that architecture and counsel approve acceptance, public surfaces collect **inquiries only**.

## Restricted vs unrestricted

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Restricted** | Use limited to purposes, costs, periods, or deliverables stated in the grant instrument |
| **Unrestricted** | Available for general mission and operations as authorized by the receiving entity’s governance, without a specific project restriction |

Restricted funds are not available for investor returns or unrelated private distributions.

## Core rules

1. Use grant funds only according to the written grant terms and applicable law.
2. Keep accounting separation where needed so restricted balances are identifiable and not mixed with unrelated capital.
3. Meet reporting, audit, and recordkeeping obligations required by the grant or by law.
4. Disclose and manage conflicts of interest before accepting or allocating grant-related decisions ([`conflict-of-interest-policy-v0.1.md`](./conflict-of-interest-policy-v0.1.md)).
5. Return or reallocate unused funds when legally or contractually required.
6. Financial support does not confer governance authority over research findings, community data, editorial positions, or the mission unless a lawful, disclosed instrument expressly provides a limited role.

## Prohibitions

- Diverting restricted funds to unauthorized purposes
- Treating grant capital as investment capital or equity-like participation
- Accepting terms that require concealment of material conflicts or surrender of mission independence

## Product behavior until acceptance is authorized

- `/fund/institutional` collects inquiries only
- No in-app grant payment checkout
- Software ledger entries, if any, are not a substitute for legal books or funder reports
