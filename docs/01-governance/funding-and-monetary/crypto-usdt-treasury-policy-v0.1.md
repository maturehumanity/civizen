# Crypto and USDT treasury policy v0.1

**Status:** Conditional / Future · **Disabled** — not Civizen’s current funding model or treasury practice. Activated only if digital-asset acceptance is expressly adopted after legal review.  
**Related:** [`funding-and-financial-integrity.md`](../../02-policies/institutional/funding-and-financial-integrity.md) · earlier constitution [Historical / Superseded](./funding-constitution-v0.1.md)  
**Counsel review:** Not started  
**Product default:** **USDT / digital asset acceptance is disabled.**

## Purpose

Provisional outline only — retained so a future adoption decision has a starting point. This document does **not** authorize acceptance, custody, or treasury use of digital assets.

## If ever adopted (not current)

Any future acceptance would require a compliant treasury process, identity and sanctions controls appropriate to the funding class, lane separation from restricted grant funds, and counsel-approved custody. Details are intentionally not expanded here.

## Prohibitions (current)

- Treating crypto/USDT as an active Civizen funding route
- In-app digital-asset donation or investment checkout
- Mixing speculative digital-asset holdings with restricted grant funds

## Open questions

Deferred until an express adoption decision. See [`open-legal-questions.md`](./open-legal-questions.md).
