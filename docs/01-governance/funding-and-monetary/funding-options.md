# Funding Options

**Purpose:** Concise comparison of funding routes that may be available to Civizen. This is a decision aid, not a selection of a winner.

**Status:** Draft. Civizen has not committed to a single financing model. Different routes may coexist. Capital acceptance still requires institutional readiness and counsel review.

**Parent strategy:** [`funding-and-sustainability-plan.md`](./funding-and-sustainability-plan.md) · **Readiness:** [`funding-readiness-roadmap.md`](./funding-readiness-roadmap.md)

**Public integrity baseline:** [`funding-and-financial-integrity.md`](../../02-policies/institutional/funding-and-financial-integrity.md)

---

## How to read this table

- **Current-stage suitability** reflects Civizen’s present founder-led, inquiry-only posture—not a judgment of long-term value.
- **Prerequisites** are indicative, not exhaustive legal requirements.
- Routes can be combined; diversification is often preferable to single-source dependence.

| Route | What it could fund | Suitability now | Advantages | Limitations | Mission / control risks | Prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Grants / foundations** | Product, research, pilots, education, community programs; often restricted | Moderate later; limited until entity, compliance, and pilot framing exist | Mission-aligned capital; credibility; restricted-purpose clarity | Competitive; reporting burden; restricted use | Agenda drift if grants reshape priorities; dependency on a few foundations | Eligible entity; proposal capacity; restricted-funds controls; reporting calendar |
| **Philanthropy** | Unrestricted or lightly restricted mission support, operations, reserves | Emerging after credible public identity; not primary focus yet | Flexible support; no equity claim when structured as gifts | Unpredictable; relationship-intensive | Donor dependency; reputational association; pressure for influence | Donation policy; receipt entity; sanctions/source-of-funds where required; no false tax claims |
| **International institutions** | Large pilots, research, capacity building, multi-country programs | Later — after institutional maturity and evidence | Scale; legitimacy; multi-jurisdiction reach | Slow process; heavy compliance; political context | Soft political capture; conditionality that conflicts with independence | Formal partnership capacity; auditability; clear independence terms |
| **Universities / research partnerships** | Research, evaluation, education content, early pilots | Relatively strong near-term fit for evidence and pilots | Credibility; talent; low capture relative to commercial capital | Limited cash; academic timelines; IP negotiation | Research independence must be protected; IP or data overreach | MoUs; ethics/data agreements; publication freedom; clear non-governance terms |
| **Government / public funding** | Public-interest programs, local pilots, civic tech deployments | Later and selective; high compliance load | Public mandate fit; scale in some jurisdictions | Procurement complexity; political cycles | Political capture; partisan association; conditionality | Eligible recipient; procurement compliance; independence safeguards |
| **Corporate sponsorship** | Events, education, named programs, communications (per agreement) | Selective once brand and deliverables are clear | Cash + visibility; partnership signaling | Brand constraints; deliverable pressure | Commercial capture; implied endorsement; quiet equity-like terms | Sponsorship policy; conflict review; written deliverables; public disclosure when material |
| **Community donations** | Modest operations, community programs, signaling support | Useful as signal; usually insufficient alone | Broad ownership signal; low governance claim when gifts | Small scale; processing costs; compliance still applies | Over-promising impact; false tax deductibility claims | Donation acceptance rules; payment rails only when authorized; transparent limits |
| **Mission-aligned private investment** | Commercial entity costs, product scale, go-to-market (if ever authorized) | Not current — inquiry only; no public offering | Growth capital; professional discipline | Dilution / control pressure; securities complexity | Investor capture; mission assets treated as commercial property | Separate commercial vehicle; counsel; offering docs; KYC; clear separation from mission assets — see [`investor-interest-non-offering-notice.md`](../../02-policies/institutional/investor-interest-non-offering-notice.md) |
| **Commercial / earned revenue** | Ongoing operations; reduces fundraising dependence | Medium-term option after product maturity; not an approved model set yet | Recurring sustainability; customer feedback | Sales capacity; may distract from civic core | Paywalling civic participation; commercial capture of roadmap | Clear product boundaries; accounting; mission-compatible pricing; no pay-to-participate core |
| **Future token / monetary mechanisms** | Uncertain — only if separately adopted | **Not current.** Conditional / Future · Disabled | Potentially novel coordination or treasury tools *if* lawful and adopted | High legal/regulatory complexity; custody; market and reputational risk | Speculative capture; regulatory exposure; confusion with money or equity | Explicit governance adoption; counsel; custody/compliance; public clarity that mechanisms are not active — see [`crypto-usdt-treasury-policy-v0.1.md`](./crypto-usdt-treasury-policy-v0.1.md) |

## Notes

1. **No winner selected.** Evidence does not yet justify committing Civizen to one primary route.
2. **Coexistence is allowed.** Grants, earned revenue, and carefully separated commercial financing can coexist if legally classified and mission-protected.
3. **Token / crypto mechanisms** are not established funding policy. Product acceptance of digital assets remains disabled until a counsel-approved policy and custody model exist.
4. **Superseded percentage pools and distribution formulas** from earlier drafts are not current options and must not be treated as terms. See the folder [README](./README.md).
5. Detailed route-specific strategy documents should be created only when Civizen actively pursues that route.
