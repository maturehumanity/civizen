**Status:** Current Draft — subject to legal and institutional review. Capital acceptance not enabled.

**Counsel review:** Not started

## Purpose

Define how Civizen may accept philanthropic support without creating investor rights.

The legal entity or entities capable of receiving donations will be determined through Civizen’s institutional and legal architecture.

## Core rules

1. Donations are **not** investments.
2. A donation must not create ownership, profit participation, repayment rights, investor status, or contributor payout rights unless a separate approved instrument says otherwise.
3. Restricted donations may only be used for the stated restriction.
4. Records must include: donor name or anonymous flag, amount, currency, date, restrictions, receipt number, refund status, public display preference, tax receipt status, and source-of-funds review where required.

## Product behavior until counsel completes this policy

- `/fund/support` collects **interest / intent** only.
- No card, wire, or crypto donation checkout in-app.
- Copy must not promise tax deductibility unless the accepting entity and counsel confirm it.
