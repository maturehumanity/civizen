# Funding Readiness Roadmap

**Purpose:** Practical stages for what Civizen should do before and while pursuing external funding. Stages are ordered by readiness, not by calendar dates.

**Status:** Draft operational guidance. Completing a stage does not authorize capital acceptance by itself.

**Parent strategy:** [`funding-and-sustainability-plan.md`](./funding-and-sustainability-plan.md) · **Route comparison:** [`funding-options.md`](./funding-options.md)

**Legal gates remain open:** The entity or entities capable of receiving grants, donations, commercial revenue, or future investment will be determined through Civizen’s institutional and legal architecture. Offering rules and acceptance controls require counsel. See [`open-legal-questions.md`](./open-legal-questions.md). Historical Phase 0 notes: [`implementation-roadmap-v0.1.md`](./implementation-roadmap-v0.1.md).

---

## Stage overview

| Stage | Name | Primary outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Foundation and credibility | Credible public project identity |
| 2 | Awareness and community | Visible ongoing development and participation |
| 3 | Pilot readiness | Concrete, measurable pilot proposals |
| 4 | Targeted funding | Selective conversations matched to real work |
| 5 | Evidence and expansion | Demonstrated outcomes and stronger partners |
| 6 | Sustainability | Diversified financing and reduced fundraising dependence |

Stages may overlap. Do not skip Stage 1–2 credibility work to chase broad fundraising.

---

## Stage 1 — Foundation and credibility

**Do:**

- Clear philosophy and purpose (foundation docs and public copy)
- Public website and accurate legal/status messaging
- Working platform under active development
- Governance and institutional documentation that distinguishes current from long-term aims
- GitHub / open-source presence
- Coherent public identity (brand, naming, institutional notices)

**Ready to advance when:**

- An unfamiliar institutional reader can understand what Civizen is and is not
- Public funding pages correctly state inquiry-only posture
- Controlling institutional policies are discoverable ([`../../02-policies/institutional/`](../../02-policies/institutional/))
- Software demonstrates continuous, substantive progress

---

## Stage 2 — Awareness and community

**Do:**

- Civizen social presence (as authorized channels exist)
- Founder communication that explains purpose without overclaiming
- LinkedIn and X presence used for clarity, not hype
- Public discussions and documentation that invite informed participation
- Contributor and community pathways (including open-source contribution)
- Visible demonstration of ongoing development

**Ready to advance when:**

- There is a repeatable public narrative people can share accurately
- Community or contributor activity is real, not only announced
- Outreach is selective and consistent with mission protection principles

---

## Stage 3 — Pilot readiness

**Do:**

- Identify concrete pilot use cases (who, where, what problem, what success looks like)
- Define resources needed (people, infrastructure, time, compliance)
- Establish measurable objectives
- Identify suitable universities, NGOs, institutions, or communities
- Prepare a realistic pilot budget only for the scoped pilot

**Ready to advance when:**

- At least one pilot concept is specific enough to brief an external partner
- Resource needs and constraints are written down
- Independence and data/privacy boundaries are clear
- Success metrics are measurable and falsifiable

---

## Stage 4 — Targeted funding

**Do:**

- Identify funders whose objectives match actual Civizen initiatives (not generic “civic tech” spray)
- Begin institutional conversations
- Pursue grants, philanthropy, partnerships, or investment **as appropriate to the initiative and entity readiness**
- Prepare funding-specific materials only when needed for a real conversation

**Ready to advance when:**

- Conversations are grounded in pilots or programs Civizen can deliver
- Legal/institutional capacity to accept the relevant funding class is understood
- Terms are screened against mission-protection principles
- Materials remain inquiry- or proposal-specific; no premature public offering claims

Public investor pages remain non-offering notices until counsel authorizes otherwise ([`investor-interest-non-offering-notice.md`](../../02-policies/institutional/investor-interest-non-offering-notice.md)).

---

## Stage 5 — Evidence and expansion

**Do:**

- Demonstrate pilot outcomes with honest reporting
- Improve the platform based on evidence
- Build institutional credibility through delivery, not promises
- Expand partnerships carefully
- Pursue larger funding where justified by results and capacity

**Ready to advance when:**

- Outcomes (or clear lessons) can be shown without exaggeration
- Partners renew or expand on the basis of delivery
- Reporting and classification practices match funding class requirements
- Expansion does not create single-source dependence

---

## Stage 6 — Sustainability

**Do:**

- Develop recurring revenue where mission-compatible (without treating options as already approved models)
- Diversify funding across compatible sources
- Establish reserves
- Reduce dependency on continuous fundraising
- Strengthen institutional independence beyond founder-led stewardship

**Ready when (ongoing condition, not a finish line):**

- Operations are not hostage to a single funder class or relationship
- Core civic participation remains free of pay-to-participate barriers
- Governance authority remains separate from financial support
- Reserves and reporting practices support continuity

## Cross-cutting gates

These apply at every stage where capital might be accepted:

| Gate | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Classification | Know the funding class before use ([`funding-and-financial-integrity.md`](../../02-policies/institutional/funding-and-financial-integrity.md)) |
| Entity | Receiving entity/entities determined through institutional/legal architecture; correct recipient for the funding class |
| Compliance | KYC/AML/sanctions and restricted-funds controls as required ([`kyc-aml-sanctions-policy-outline-v0.1.md`](./kyc-aml-sanctions-policy-outline-v0.1.md)) |
| Conflicts | Disclose and manage conflicts ([`conflict-of-interest-policy-v0.1.md`](./conflict-of-interest-policy-v0.1.md)) |
| Transparency | Publish only what policy and law allow ([`public-transparency-ledger-policy-v0.1.md`](./public-transparency-ledger-policy-v0.1.md)) |
| No fixed public pools | Do not publish investor/contributor/founder percentage formulas |

---

## What this roadmap is not

- Not a fundraising campaign calendar
- Not a commitment to investment rounds
- Not authorization to accept capital
- Not a replacement for counsel-approved instruments
- Not a reason to create separate grant/investor/commercial strategy docs before those routes are actively pursued
