# Civizen Terms of Use

**Terms version:** `2026-08-01-purpose-alignment-v1`

## 1. Scope and acceptance

These Terms of Use govern participation in Civizen as a digital platform, civic environment, and internal governance ecosystem. They apply only after a user affirmatively accepts the identified Terms version during account creation or a later re-consent flow. Remaining registered, staying signed in, or continuing to browse Civizen does not constitute acceptance of material changes.

## 2. Nature of the Service

Civizen currently operates as a digital platform and voluntary civic environment supporting trust-building, contribution, study, civic participation, cooperation, and structured governance. Civizen may maintain internal rules, constitutional structures, moderation systems, and participation requirements that govern behavior and rights inside the platform.

## 3. Code of Conduct

When you use or join Civizen, you explicitly agree to participate in a community built on honesty and mutual respect. Civizen is a place free from lies, fraud, impersonation, misleading activity, and disrespect toward others.

By joining, you accept this standard—not only for what you will not do, but for how you will treat others: with the same care, honesty, and fairness you expect for yourself. This code of conduct applies across messaging, profiles, marketplace activity, endorsements, governance participation, and every other part of the Civizen ecosystem.

## 4. Accounts and Truthfulness

Users must provide truthful, accurate, and non-deceptive information. Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and for activity taken through their accounts unless and until compromise is reported through Civizen procedures.

## 5. Verification

Certain rights and features require identity and contact verification. Verification may include structured profile completion, live in-app digital verification, discrepancy review, and anti-duplication checks. Verification may be suspended, denied, or revoked where legitimacy cannot be safely established.

## 6. Citizenship and Civic Participation

Civizen may recognize current civic statuses such as citizen, active citizen, and governance-eligible citizen under separate policies. These statuses presently govern participation within Civizen and do not by themselves create government-recognized nationality, immigration status, or public-law citizenship.

Civizen's broader public mission may include research, advocacy, institution-building, and lawful efforts toward future recognition of planetary citizenship. These Terms do not themselves grant that recognition or public authority.

## 7. Governance Eligibility

Governance rights are conditional. Civizen may require verified identity, citizenship status, active status, minimum Civizen score, time in good standing, role-specific training, certification, or trusted device requirements before governance permissions are granted or exercised.

## 8. Contributions, Recognition, and Compensation

Everyone is invited to contribute in their own capacity — through skills, knowledge, civic participation, verification, moderation, development, or other meaningful work inside the Civizen ecosystem.

Recognition may include attribution, contribution records, badges, credentials, and community responsibilities. Participation does not automatically create employment, wages, equity, royalties, revenue participation, or a right to payment. Compensation exists only under an approved employment relationship, contract, grant, fellowship, bounty, prize, or other written program.

Users remain responsible for the accuracy and integrity of the information, endorsements, comments, legal suggestions, and other content they contribute.

## 9. Content and Conduct

Users must act honestly, avoid abuse, and refrain from deception, exploitation, harassment, fraud, or manipulation, consistent with the Code of Conduct in Section 3. Civizen may review, limit, organize, remove, or restrict content and conduct to preserve safety, legitimacy, and institutional integrity.

## 10. Privacy and Sensitive Data

Civizen must protect personal and verification data through strict access controls, encryption, segmentation, and auditable handling. Sensitive verification records should be stored off-chain, while public proofs, hashes, attestations, and revocation states may be anchored in more transparent systems where appropriate. Raw identity documents must not be placed on-chain.

## 11. Authorized Access

Founder, staff, reviewers, and later civic stewards may access only what is necessary to fulfill authorized duties. Private communications, detailed personal information, and financial privacy data are private by default and may only be accessed where consent, incident procedure, or defined authority permits. Sensitive access should be logged wherever possible.

## 12. Enforcement and Appeals

Civizen may limit visibility, restrict actions, suspend privileges, remove content, revoke rights, or restrict accounts where conduct or contribution conflicts with Civizen's rules, safety requirements, or civic integrity standards. Civizen should also maintain appeal and review pathways appropriate to the severity and type of action taken.

## 13. Internal Civic Order

Civizen may maintain a Community Governance Charter, citizenship policy, verification policy, governance permission model, Community Readiness and Program Availability Framework, and role maturity framework. Those materials govern specific classes of internal rights and duties inside Civizen and should be interpreted consistently with these Terms of Use.

## 14. Changes

Civizen may update these Terms. Material changes require clear notice and, where required, renewed affirmative acceptance of the newly identified Terms version before protected use continues under that version. Editorial corrections that do not change substantive rights or duties may be published without re-consent. Remaining registered, staying signed in, or continued browsing does not by itself constitute acceptance of material changes.
