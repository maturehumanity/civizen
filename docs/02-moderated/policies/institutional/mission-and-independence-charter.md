# Mission and Independence Charter

## Mission

The mission of Mature Humanity and Civizen is to help humanity develop more peaceful, responsible, cooperative, transparent, and sustainable ways of living and working together.

Civizen seeks to unite people around a shared identity as citizens of humanity and to develop practical foundations for a peaceful, accountable, and mature planetary civilization.

The initiative supports:

- protection of human dignity,
- peaceful cooperation,
- responsible stewardship of natural and human resources,
- access to knowledge and opportunity,
- transparent and accountable institutions,
- open public-interest technology,
- cultural understanding,
- non-discrimination,
- human-centered development,
- and long-term thinking.

## Current Voluntary and Non-Governmental Stage

At this stage, Civizen is a voluntary civic and public-interest initiative.

It does not claim sovereignty, territorial jurisdiction, police power, taxation authority, diplomatic recognition, or authority to replace national or local law.

Civizen’s community rules apply only within the scope of participation in the platform and its programs.

## Long-Term Planetary Citizenship Aim

Civizen is not intended to remain merely a private membership label or isolated software platform. Its long-term aim is to help people build the shared principles, trust, democratic participation, institutional capacity, and lawful recognition needed for a complementary form of planetary citizenship.

Civizen does not claim that this status already exists. It seeks to create a responsible pathway through which humanity and legitimate institutions may decide whether and how such citizenship should become formally recognized.

## Political Independence

Civizen is not a political party and does not exist to place any political candidate, government, nation, religion, corporation, donor, investor, or ideology in control of the initiative.

Public education, research, policy discussion, and institutional cooperation must remain distinguishable from partisan campaign activity.

## Protection Against Capture

No donor, investor, sponsor, government, company, platform participant, founder, employee, or partner may purchase or privately acquire ownership of the mission.

Funding does not create:

- control over research findings,
- control over community decisions,
- ownership of charitable or public-interest assets,
- access to private user information,
- authority to direct political activity,
- or exemption from ethics, conflict, transparency, and accountability rules.

## Human Rights and Equal Dignity

Participation and institutional decisions must respect human dignity and must not discriminate unlawfully on the basis of identity, origin, nationality, race, ethnicity, religion, sex, disability, age, political viewpoint, or other protected status.

Differences of opinion may be debated. Deception, harassment, threats, coercion, exploitation, and dehumanization are not protected as constructive participation.

## Transparency with Legitimate Limits

The initiative supports public transparency in governance, funding, institutional relationships, policies, and material decisions.

Transparency does not require disclosure of:

- personal information,
- private communications,
- security-sensitive information,
- confidential legal advice,
- protected donor information,
- employment records,
- unpublished negotiations,
- trade secrets,
- or information whose disclosure would create harm or violate law.

## Open Participation

People and institutions may contribute through research, education, software, design, translation, review, testing, public discussion, institutional programs, and other documented forms of service.

Participation does not automatically create employment, compensation, ownership, governance authority, or intellectual property rights beyond those expressly provided in applicable terms or written agreements.

## Continuity Beyond the Founder

The founder’s initiating role and authorship are recognized as part of the initiative’s history.

The mission, official records, institutional assets, and public-interest obligations must be capable of continuing beyond any individual.

No critical institutional function should remain permanently dependent on one person, one credential, one private account, or one unreviewable decision path.
