# Citizenship and Verification Policy v0.1

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how a person moves from signup to verified status, citizenship, active citizenship, and governance eligibility inside Civizen.

## 2. Verification Requirements

Verification may require:

- complete personal information submission
- complete contact information submission
- live in-app digital verification
- discrepancy review
- anti-duplication and legitimacy checks

Verification alone does not grant citizenship, active status, or governance rights.

## 3. Data Protection

Civizen should protect sensitive identity data through:

- strong encryption at rest
- least-privilege access
- segmented storage
- auditable access handling
- off-chain storage of raw identity materials
- on-chain or public anchoring only for hashes, attestations, proofs, and revocation states where appropriate

## 4. Citizenship

A verified member may become a citizen by:

- holding a verified account in good standing for 30 days, or
- manually accepting citizenship after 14 days where review is complete and no blocking discrepancy exists

Citizenship means acceptance of Civizen's internal civic framework, principles, rules, code of conduct, and related institutional procedures for participation inside the Civizen system. At the current stage, Civizen citizenship is a voluntary civic status within the Civizen network. It is not yet a government-recognized nationality, immigration status, or legal citizenship, and the status alone does not create those rights.

## Long-Term Citizenship Development

Civizen citizenship is intended to help people practice and build a shared civic identity as citizens of humanity. As participation, legitimacy, institutional capacity, safeguards, and lawful cooperation develop, Civizen may pursue pathways through which planetary citizenship could receive broader social, institutional, and legal recognition. No profile status alone creates that recognition.

## 5. Active Status

Verified and citizen badges are distinct from active status. A person may be a citizen but not yet active until published participation and program-readiness criteria for the relevant Civizen scope are met under the Community Readiness and Program Availability Framework. Active status currently records participation and program readiness within Civizen. It does not by itself create legal or governmental authority.

## 6. Governance Eligibility

Governance eligibility requires more than citizenship. At minimum, Civizen may require:

- verified status
- citizen status
- active status where program readiness applies
- minimum Civizen score
- minimum time in good standing
- no serious unresolved sanctions
- trusted governance action path, including native mobile app requirements where adopted
- role-specific training or certification where applicable

## 7. Suspension and Revocation

Verification, active status, and governance eligibility may be suspended or revoked under appropriate procedures. Governance eligibility should generally be easier to suspend than citizenship itself, while still remaining reviewable and appealable.
