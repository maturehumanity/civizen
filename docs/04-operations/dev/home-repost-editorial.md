# Home posts — Civizen vs personal (editorial pattern)

**Core principle:** The source publishes the idea. People add perspective. Civizen preserves the relationship.

## Civizen account

Use for:

- philosophical positions and principles
- major ideas and system proposals
- project announcements and opportunities
- institutional developments
- research and findings

Civizen speaks as the **system/project**.

## Personal accounts

Prefer **Repost → Repost with thoughts** when adding:

- founder perspective
- professional interpretation
- personal experience
- disagreement or critique
- why the original topic matters to them

Commentary stays on the personal post; the canonical Civizen body stays on one `posts` row, linked via `post_reposts.original_post_id` (never paste the full Civizen body into the personal post).

## Standalone personal posts

Appropriate when the subject originates with that person and is **not** primarily a response to an existing Civizen post (diary, personal announcement, original idea not yet published by Civizen).

## Feed presentation

- Quote-reposts show commentary first, then a compact **Reposted from…** embed (≈2–4 lines + See full post).
- Do not duplicate the full original in the feed card.
- Chronology follows real `created_at` values; do not invent timestamps to force visual order.

## Historical integrity

When correcting authorship or linking existing posts:

- prefer `UPDATE` / relationship insert over duplicating content
- preserve dates, likes, comments, and external-publication metadata
- do **not** silently publish unreleased personal reflections on someone’s behalf
