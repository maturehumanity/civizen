# Five-year ecosystem cost reconciliation v0.1

**Purpose:** Audit and rebuild the civilization-scale **five-year** cost picture. The ~**$4B base** five-year ecosystem total in `09` is **internally inconsistent** with a serious multi-jurisdiction reading of the same document’s $40M/jurisdiction unit and is **likely undercount**. This reconciliation does **not** preserve $4B because it already appeared in `09`.

**Hypotheses tested (not ceilings):**

| Hypothesis band | Five-year ecosystem investment |
| --- | ---: |
| Constrained ecosystem | **$15–25B** |
| Realistic base | **$30–50B** (point estimate below **~$35B**) |
| Average annual (base) | **~$7B/year** |
| Accelerated ecosystem | **$75–150B+** |

**Database:** No schema or amount changes. Draft Budget remains **0** / draft / demonstration / unapproved / unpublished.

**Relationship to `09`:** Capability map, launch gates, and operator *concept* in `09` remain useful. **Ecosystem monetary totals in `09` are preliminary and superseded for five-year planning by this document.**

## 0. What the former ~$4B base actually covered

### 0.1 Reconstructing `09`’s five-year base arithmetic

From `09` §6.5 and the generating assumptions:

| Component | Approx. amount | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Global-core (5y proxy ≈ 75% of ~$1.2B lifecycle) | **~$0.9B** | Discovery+build+stewardship compressed |
| 100 operators: setup + 5y OpEx | **~$0.68B** | $80M setup + 5×$120M |
| Jurisdictions | **~$0.8B** | **Only ~20** × $40M — **not ~200** |
| “Institutional” add-on | **~$1.2B** | Flat **1.5×** jurisdiction IT — not institution types |
| Reserves on core+ops only | **~$0.4B** | 25% of core+ops; **not** on J/institutions |
| **Total** | **~$4B** | |

### 0.2 What ~$4B therefore meant

A **thin first-wave portfolio**: ~20 medium-style jurisdiction IT packages, a crude institutional multiplier, a 100-operator network, and a partial global-core — **not**:

- worldwide or ~200-jurisdiction deployment;
- explicit courts, tax, banks, employers, universities, CSOs, auditors, multilaterals;
- full payments/tax/economic infrastructure programs;
- scaled adoption, assisted access, grants/capacity;
- adequate reserves against J/institutional failure;
- multi-year cash-flow realism.

### 0.3 Material omissions / undercounts relative to civilization-scale intent

1. **Jurisdiction count:** 20 vs first-wave seriousness (50–80+) or worldwide (~200). At $40M × 200 ≈ **$8B jurisdictions alone** before other layers.  
2. **Institutional integrations** as first-class costs (often **≥** jurisdiction IT).  
3. **Layer separation:** payments/tax, audit/disputes, a11y/l10n/education, adoption/change, grants, independent oversight collapsed or missing.  
4. **Support/24×7** at ecosystem scale underweighted.  
5. **Reserves** not applied to the dominant J/institutional spend.  
6. **Workforce** outside global-core + operators (J and institutional FTE) largely invisible.  
7. **Uneven annual cash** (Year 5 ≫ Year 1) flattened into a single lump.

**Verdict on $4B:** Useful only as a **minimum exploratory first-wave IT-centric sketch**. **Not** a realistic base for the intended five-year civilization-scale program.

**Honesty rule:** Do **not** describe this outcome as serving all humanity.

## 10. Required conclusions

### 1. What did the former $4B base actually cover?
A thin first-wave sketch: partial global-core, 100 operators for 5 years, **~20** jurisdictions × $40M, a flat 1.5× “institutional” fudge, and reserves on core+ops only.

### 2. What material costs did it omit?
Most jurisdictions; first-class institutional/legacy integrations; explicit payments/tax/audit/dispute/a11y/adoption/grants/oversight layers; adequate portfolio reserves; ecosystem workforce outside core/operators; realistic Year 4–5 cash ramp.

### 3. Is ~$35B over five years supported as a realistic base?
**Yes, as a midpoint hypothesis** under §1 outcomes and §2 additive layers (**~$35.4B** calculated), landing in **$30–50B**. Confidence **low–medium** until professional studies and quotes. Not a ceiling or a commitment.

### 4. What would ~$35B plausibly produce after five years?
~70 participating J; **15–25** limited-production J for defined subsets; 100–150 operators; federation/identity/privacy/security baselines in production; selected civic and admin pilots; tens of millions active users; 1–2B residents only **potentially** in scope — **not** all humanity served.

### 5. What would it still not produce?
Worldwide mature ecosystem; ~200 J production; universal civic authority; complete tax/justice/payments stacks; recognized planetary citizenship as public law; billion-scale active usage.

### 6. What portion is global-core vs broader ecosystem?
Roughly **~$5–8B global-core organization** vs **~$1–2B operators** vs **~$20–25B J/institutions** within ~$35B total. **Total ecosystem ≠ one entity’s budget.**

### 7. Minimum multi-year committed capitalization before major implementation?
Order **~$2–4B committed** up front (runway + operator seed + early reserves + first-wave matches), with a credible tranche path through Years 1–3 — **not** hoping Year 2 appears.

### 8. Annual funding/ops after Year 5?
Often **~$2–5B+/year** combined (core + operators + live J/institutions), scaling with production footprint.
