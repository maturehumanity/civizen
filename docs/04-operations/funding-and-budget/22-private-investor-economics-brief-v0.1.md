## 1. Investable opportunity (narrow)

Return-seeking capital, if ever accepted, belongs in **commercial services entities** and **operator/infrastructure vehicles** — not in the public-interest steward’s restricted funds, independent oversight budgets, or citizen-essential infrastructure priced for profit.

## 3. Permitted revenue sources

Enterprise APIs; institutional integrations; implementation/migration; managed hosting (often operator vehicle); operator certification/support; optional enterprise workflows; flagged B2B marketplace aids; training/TA; open-compatible licensing; availability payments; lawful institutional support tiers.

**Not profit:** personal-capacity transaction fees (always $0); legal-entity processing fees (cost-recovery only).

**Prohibited:** personal-data sales; selling authority; civic-vote control; mandatory-identity profit; citizen payment profit; covert ads/surveillance; diverting taxes/restricted funds; pay-to-govern.

## 6. Potential returns and payback (illustrative)

Payout hypothesis: **10%** of eligible **distributable commercial CF** to an investor pool (tested vs 5% and 15%; **not adopted policy**).

| Illustration (base, ~$800M common equity share) | Payback | MOIC | IRR |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Cash distributions only (no exit TV) | None in 15y | ~0.3× | negative |
| With speculative terminal value at Y15 | ~Y15 | ~5× | ~15% |

**Implication:** under mission-compatible waterfall discipline, **equity payback inside 15 years usually needs an exit/terminal-value assumption** or a **smaller equity check** paired with debt/preferred/capped RR/operator PF. Treat upside as speculative.

## 7. Major risks

Adoption slower than modeled; regulatory limits on commercial streams; security-cost spikes; delayed launch; jurisdiction withdrawal; FX/inflation; no private capital; no TV exit; entity/securities unreadiness (**D6**); mission-conflict pressure.

Stress response: **reduce / suspend / accrue** pools (step-down toward 5%/5% or zero).

---

## 8. Mission protections

Separate commercial vs public funds; no investor ownership of citizen data; no civic voting rights from investment; ownership caps; mission lock; independent oversight funding protected; no payout from restricted grants; no payout below continuity reserve policy; clawback for fraud/misrepresentation.
