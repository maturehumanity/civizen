## B. FAQ — short answers

### B1. How much are you raising?

**Now:** Validation program — same-scope low ~$552.4M / Recommended base ~$634M (exact $634.4M) / high ~$833.2M (`33` / app draft v0.3). First committed tranche ~$132M; initial received/escrowed floor provisionally $60M. Constrained (~$374M) and Expanded (~$1.03B) = different scopes. Historical superseded: $530.2M · $524M · $446M.  
**Primary program context (not the immediate ask):** preliminary five-year first-wave ecosystem plan (`11`) — ~$30–50B range, modeled base ~$37.5B (~$37.5–38B hypothesis).  
**Not ordinary intro content:** Years 6–20 long-range **scenarios** (`13`) — low-confidence lifecycle outlooks, not budgets.

### B2. Is $37.5B your budget? What about longer horizons?

No. The five-year figure is a **preliminary ecosystem-wide hypothesis** across many parties — not a single-organization budget and not verified. Longer illustrative scenario totals exist only as **internal lifecycle stress tests** with low confidence; they are **not** budgets, forecasts, promises, or current requests, and have **not** been accepted by governments, funders, or institutions.

### B3. What does the money pay for first?

Independent panels, system-catalog validation, priority domain studies, constitutional/institutional design, architecture/security/privacy/identity/AI-governance/economic feasibility, consultations, accessibility/localization, non-authoritative demos, cost-model validation, organizational formation, and a safe-pause reserve for **this** phase.

### B4. What if later build funding never arrives?

Validation outputs (reports, standards drafts, reviewed architecture, domain volumes, dissent records, phase accounts) are designed to remain independently useful.

### B5. Who controls the funds?

Validation Recommended Base split (approx.): core-controlled ~$437.8M; independent review ~$36.0M (ring-fenced); grants/pass-through ~$94.0M; protected reserve ~$66.6M. Five-year ecosystem capital is majority **not** core-controlled.

### B6. Are you a government / creating a country?

No. Pathway materials describe **voluntary** stages first. Recognized planetary citizenship and statehood are later, contested questions — not current claims.

### B7. Can I invest for equity / token / return?

Do not invent product. Response: *We are not offering securities, tokens, or investment returns in this inquiry stage. Depending on counsel-approved vehicles, future instruments may be philanthropic, grant, or other — none are approved for acceptance yet.*

### B8. Where is your audited budget in the app?

The in-app ordinary Budget selector defaults to **`Civizen Pre-Major-Build Validation Program v0.3`** (exact Recommended Base **$634,400,000.00**; draft/unapproved/unpublished). Superseded v0.2 / v0.1 remain in history, not primary selector clutter. The obsolete Draft Budget demonstration skeleton (`Civizen Draft Budget v0.1`, zeros) was **retired** from ordinary application use. Public finance pages only show approved-and-published budgets. Settings → Funding → **Program plan** shows read-only planning summaries (not public).

### B9. How do tranche releases work?

Proposed: T0 inquiry readiness → T1–T3 validation under gates → T4 continuity **only if** validation gates pass. Gate failure means revise, narrow, or pause — not rebrand demos as production.

### B10. How accurate are the dollar figures?

Validation figures are **working estimates**. Five-year figures are **preliminary hypotheses**. Long-range scenario figures are **low-confidence** internal outlooks. Independent cost validation is itself a funded workstream.

### B11. What about climate / health / education systems?

Domain work begins with **validation studies** and frameworks, not claims that all systems are fully built or fully costed. Inventory entries (~467) are living hypotheses pending review.

### B12. Can you take my money this week?

Unless and until owner + counsel approve a receiving entity and acceptance controls: **No.** We can continue an inquiry and share the brief (`16`) and diligence index (`19`).
