# Civizen external funding brief v0.1

**Status:** Inquiry-readiness brief — **not** a solicitation, prospectus, securities offering, grant application, or approved budget.  
**Confidence:** Working estimates and hypotheses pending independent validation and owner instruments.  
**Application finance records:** Ordinary Budget selection centers the validation subprogram draft. Obsolete Draft Budget v0.1 demonstration fixture is **retired** from ordinary use and is **not** the program funding ask.

**Immediate proposition:** fund an **18–24 month pre-major-build validation and institutional formation program** that produces independently valuable public goods **without** irreversible production obligations.

**Inquiry sentence (current):** Civizen’s recommended 18–24 month pre-major-build validation program currently has a provisional Base of approximately $634M, with a same-scope planning range of approximately $552–833M pending quotations and professional validation. This is separate from the approximately $37.5–38B five-year first-wave implementation hypothesis.

Historical progression (superseded estimates, retained for provenance): Validation Program v0.1 Base **~$446M**; early v0.2 proposal **~$524M**; completed v0.2 coverage draft **exact $530.2M**. Constrained (~$374M) and Expanded (~$1.03B) cases in `33` are **different scopes** — not equally capable cheaper/same versions of the Recommended program.

**What changes among same-scope scenarios (summary):** Low/High reflect unit-cost and quotation variance for the Recommended deliverable set. Always present the Base with the same-scope range. Do **not** claim guaranteed sufficiency. VAL-EX16 (program insurance planning allowance) remains **quote-dependent**.

**Funding stages (not a Budget table dashboard):** Distinguish expression of interest · pledge · conditional commitment · legally binding commitment · received cash · escrowed cash · available cash. First required legal commitment ~**$132M** (T1). Initial received/escrowed working floor provisionally **$60M**. Do not begin T1 on nonbinding interest alone. Insurance binding or an approved binding path before field activity. No obligations dependent on an uncommitted later tranche. Receiving-entity path unresolved — do not accept funds until authorized.

This is **not** the cost of completing Civizen worldwide, deploying ~200 jurisdictions, or funding mature global operations. This brief is **not** an investment offer and does **not** imply that validation funding generates private returns.

## 2. Problem in one paragraph

Civilization-scale civic infrastructure that touches identity, rights, administration, and many life domains cannot be improvised into production. Premature build creates lock-in, capture risk, and harm. Planning models and system inventories exist as **structured hypotheses**; they require multidisciplinary review before major construction capital is committed.

---

## 3. What validation funding buys

- Constitutional and institutional design with anti-capture safeguards  
- Sixteen independent review panels (ring-fenced)  
- Validation of the system catalog (~467 entries today; living)  
- Ten priority professional domain studies  
- Architecture, security, privacy, identity, AI-governance, and economic-feasibility work  
- Jurisdiction and institutional consultations  
- Accessibility, localization, and nondigital inclusion design  
- Controlled **non-authoritative** demonstrators (synthetic / authorized test data only)  
- Cost-model validation and organizational formation  
- Safe-pause / wind-down reserve for **this** phase  

**Independently valuable if later implementation funding is delayed:** published standards and reports, dissent-preserving catalog, reviewed architecture, domain volumes, stoppable demos, transparent phase accounting.
