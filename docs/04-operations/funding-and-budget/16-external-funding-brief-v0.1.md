# Civizen external funding brief v0.1

**Status:** Inquiry-readiness brief — **not** a solicitation, prospectus, securities offering, grant application, or approved budget.  
**Confidence:** Working estimates and hypotheses pending independent validation and owner instruments.  
**Application finance records:** Ordinary Budget selection centers the validation subprogram draft. Obsolete Draft Budget v0.1 demonstration fixture is **retired** from ordinary use and is **not** the program funding ask.

**Primary planning horizons in this brief:** (1) **18–24-month validation and institutional-formation program**; (2) **preliminary five-year first-wave ecosystem plan**. Longer horizons are **not** ordinary content here — see due diligence (`19`) and scenario analysis (`13`) when a specific long-horizon question requires them.

---

## 1. What Civizen is asking for now

**Immediate proposition:** fund an **18–24 month pre-major-build validation and institutional formation program** that produces independently valuable public goods **without** irreversible production obligations.

| Scenario | Indicative cash (USD) | Source |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Low | ~$438.3M | `30`/`31` / Validation Budget v0.2 |
| **Base (working)** | **~$530M** (exact **$530,200,000.00**) | **`30`/`31`** / app draft v0.2 |
| High | ~$654.5M | `30`/`31` / Validation Budget v0.2 |

Historical Validation Program v0.1 Base was **~$446M** (range ~$202M–$898M in `14`) — retained as superseded estimate, not the current working ask.

**What changes among scenarios (summary):** Low assumes lean staffing, fewer consultations, thinner contingency, and slower panel cadence. Base funds the full validation workstream set in `14` at planned intensity. High expands consultations, demonstrators, contingency, and assurance depth when scope or risk rises. Always present the base with the full range.

This is **not** the cost of completing Civizen worldwide, deploying ~200 jurisdictions, or funding mature global operations.

---

## 2. Problem in one paragraph

Civilization-scale civic infrastructure that touches identity, rights, administration, and many life domains cannot be improvised into production. Premature build creates lock-in, capture risk, and harm. Planning models and system inventories exist as **structured hypotheses**; they require multidisciplinary review before major construction capital is committed.

---

## 3. What validation funding buys

- Constitutional and institutional design with anti-capture safeguards  
- Sixteen independent review panels (ring-fenced)  
- Validation of the system catalog (~467 entries today; living)  
- Ten priority professional domain studies  
- Architecture, security, privacy, identity, AI-governance, and economic-feasibility work  
- Jurisdiction and institutional consultations  
- Accessibility, localization, and nondigital inclusion design  
- Controlled **non-authoritative** demonstrators (synthetic / authorized test data only)  
- Cost-model validation and organizational formation  
- Safe-pause / wind-down reserve for **this** phase  

**Independently valuable if later implementation funding is delayed:** published standards and reports, dissent-preserving catalog, reviewed architecture, domain volumes, stoppable demos, transparent phase accounting.

## 6. Proposed funding tranches (validation-first)

| Tranche | Timing | Purpose | Indicative scale (base framing) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **T0 — Inquiry / formation** | Immediate | Entity counsel path, CoI, inquiry CRM hygiene, DD pack | Low seven to low eight figures (owner-scoped) |
| **T1 — Validation start** | Months 0–6 | Program office, panels kickoff, inventory method, early studies | ~20–30% of validation envelope |
| **T2 — Validation deepen** | Months 6–18 | Studies, architecture/security, consultations, demos | ~40–50% |
| **T3 — Validation close / gates** | Months 18–24 | Cost validation report, readiness gates V-G1…V-G12, safe-pause escrow | Remainder + reserve |
| **T4 — Continuity (only if gates pass)** | After validation | Production continuity package (`11` §7.2) | Separate instruments; ~$2–4B class |

Tranche releases should be **gate-conditioned**. Failure → revise, narrow, or pause — not relabel demos as production.
