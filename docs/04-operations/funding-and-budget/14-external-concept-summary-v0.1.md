# Civizen pre-major-build validation — concept summary

**Status:** Informal public concept note. Not a funding solicitation, prospectus, or claim of verified program costs.

## The problem

Civilization-scale digital infrastructure that touches identity, rights, public administration, and many life domains cannot be improvised into production. Premature construction creates lock-in, capture risk, and harm when systems fail or are abused.

Civizen’s long-term aim is a legitimate pathway toward shared civic infrastructure for humanity. That aim does not authorize skipping independent review.

## Why validation must precede production

Existing planning artifacts (system inventories, multi-year cost frameworks) are **structured hypotheses**. They must be challenged by multidisciplinary experts, affected communities, and independent reviewers before irreversible production obligations begin.

Validation funding should purchase **reviewable public goods**, not a blank check for worldwide deployment.

## The work program (18–24 months)

- Constitutional and institutional design with anti-capture safeguards  
- Independent review panels across law, security, infrastructure, domains, and finance  
- Validation of the system catalog (necessity, rights, architecture boundaries)  
- Priority professional domain studies (health, justice, tax, payments, elections, migration, social protection, land/indigenous rights, energy, statistics)  
- Architecture, privacy, security, cryptography, AI governance, and identity interop design  
- Jurisdiction and institutional consultations  
- Accessibility, language, and nondigital inclusion  
- Controlled demonstrators using synthetic or authorized test data only  
- Cost-model validation and organizational formation  

## Expected public goods (even if later work pauses)

Published charter drafts and rights analyses; an openly versioned system catalog that preserves dissent; architecture and threat-model reports; domain study volumes; standards sketches and test-suite seeds; labeled, stoppable demonstrators; transparent phase accounting.

## Governance protections

Ring-fenced independent review funding; conflict-of-interest rules; whistleblower channels; minority reports preserved; no unilateral control by a single founder, funder, government, company, or operator; clear bans on authoritative production, real-money custody, and binding elections during this phase.

## Funding range (planning only)

Indicative cash requirement for the validation program: on the order of **~$200M (low) · ~$450M (base) · ~$900M (high)** over 18–24 months, pending quotations. This is **not** the cost of completing Civizen worldwide, and it is distinct from later production continuity capitalization.

## Participation opportunities

Independent panel seats (by expertise, not celebrity); research consortia for domain studies; civil-society and community validation grants; jurisdiction consultation pathways; standards and accessibility partners.

## What this program does not claim to deliver

- Recognized planetary citizenship or statehood  
- Production systems of record for populations  
- Completion of all civilization domains  
- Deployment across ~200 jurisdictions  
- Verified truth of prior multi-decade cost totals  
- A single organization controlling worldwide civic infrastructure
