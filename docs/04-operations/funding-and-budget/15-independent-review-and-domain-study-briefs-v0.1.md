# Independent review panels and domain study briefs v0.1

**Status:** Terms of reference sketches for commissioning — **not** appointments, awards, or named participants.  
**Parent program:** `14-pre-major-build-validation-program-v0.1.md`.  
**Database:** Unchanged.

Do **not** invent named participants. Seats are described by role and sector only.

---

## Shared panel operating rules

Apply to every panel unless a brief tightens them.

| Topic | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Independence | Ring-fenced budget (WS-16); chairs not reporting line of delivery leads for gate sign-off |
| Conflicts | Written CoI; recusal; no equity in operators under review; funder influence disclosed |
| Decision relationship | **Advisory** to Civizen decision-makers; gate evidence may require “independent acceptance,” not steward self-certification |
| Disagreement | Minority reports published alongside majority; no forced consensus language |
| Public vs confidential | Default public summaries; confidential annexes only for security-sensitive or personal data — with retention limits |
| Schedule | Kickoff by Month 2; interim Month 10; final Month 20–22; ad-hoc on inventory waves |
| Staffing (indicative) | Chair 0.2–0.4 FTE; 8–14 members; shared secretariat pool |
| Cost (indicative share of WS-16) | Roughly **1/16** of panel program ± complexity uplift (justice/security/health higher) |

## B. Priority domain study briefs

Commissioned under **WS-12**. Each study must publish a public-interest volume (minus lawful confidential annexes). Indicative cost ranges are **per study**, USD.

### Shared study requirements

- Multidisciplinary team + affected-community consultation plan  
- Sample **multiple** legal traditions (not one OECD default)  
- Explicit answers to inventory decisions for that domain  
- Independence from Civizen marketing claims  
- Duration typically **9–15 months** after kickoff  
- Dependencies on panels A1–A3 for rights/security baselines

## C. Relationship to decision-making

| Output | Binding effect |
| --- | --- |
| Panel majority report | Evidence toward gates V-G1–V-G12; not automatic approval |
| Minority report | Must be published and addressed in steward response |
| Domain study | Required input to V-G7; may force inventory/cost revision |
| Steward disagreement | Allowed only with written reasons and independent rebuttal opportunity |

No funder, founder, or operator may suppress minority reports as a condition of financing.
