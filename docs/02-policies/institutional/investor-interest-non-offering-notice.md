# Investor Interest and Non-Offering Notice

This page allows prospective institutional or private investors to request information about possible future commercial financing involving Civizen.

This page is not:

- an offer to sell a security,
- a solicitation to buy a security,
- an offering memorandum,
- a term sheet,
- a crowdfunding campaign,
- an investment contract,
- a promise of returns,
- a valuation,
- an allocation reservation,
- or an acceptance of funds.

No investment is accepted through this form.

Submitting an inquiry does not create:

- investor status,
- ownership,
- repayment rights,
- revenue participation,
- governance rights,
- rights in the Mature Humanity mission,
- rights in donations or grants,
- rights in charitable or public-interest assets,
- or an obligation for Civizen to proceed with a transaction.

Any future investment opportunity must be offered by a specifically identified legal entity, under separately provided documents, and only in jurisdictions and to persons for whom the transaction is lawfully available.

Prospective investors must rely only on final transaction documents and their own legal, tax, accounting, and financial advisers.

Do not submit bank credentials, wallet credentials, government identification documents, or other sensitive financial information through this form.
