# AI Advisory and Human Authority

## Purpose

AI may help Civizen make information more understandable, accessible, reviewable, and useful.

AI exists to support human judgment, not to replace human responsibility.

## Permitted AI Functions

AI may assist with:

- research,
- summarization,
- translation,
- accessibility,
- drafting,
- classification,
- simulation,
- anomaly detection,
- fraud indicators,
- pattern analysis,
- document organization,
- technical support,
- and decision support.

## Prohibited Autonomous Authority

AI may not independently:

- hold legal office,
- vote,
- sign contracts,
- transfer funds,
- issue securities,
- mint or destroy a financial instrument,
- determine compensation,
- hire or terminate a person,
- impose a material sanction,
- approve identity verification,
- decide an appeal,
- disclose protected information,
- change governing documents,
- or authorize emergency powers.

## Human Approval

A human with identified authority remains responsible for any decision that materially affects:

- rights,
- access,
- safety,
- privacy,
- identity,
- money,
- employment,
- reputation,
- governance,
- or legal obligations.

## Notice and Explanation

Where AI materially assists a decision, Civizen should disclose that assistance when appropriate and provide:

- the purpose of the AI use,
- the information considered,
- material limitations,
- the responsible human decision-maker,
- and an available review or appeal path where appropriate.

## Data Protection

AI systems must not receive personal, confidential, restricted, or security-sensitive information unless the use is authorized, necessary, protected, and consistent with applicable privacy and security requirements.

## No AI Personhood or Ownership

An AI system is not a citizen, director, officer, employee, contributor with legal rights, owner, beneficiary, or investor.

Outputs are reviewed and attributed according to the human and institutional process that authorized their use.

## Controlling Principle

> AI advises. Humans decide and remain accountable.
