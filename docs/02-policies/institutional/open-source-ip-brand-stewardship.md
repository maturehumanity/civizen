# Open Source, Intellectual Property, and Brand Stewardship

## Open Development

Civizen is developed with an open-source and public-audit orientation.

Source code, documentation, and governance materials may be published so that people can inspect, test, discuss, improve, and independently evaluate the system.

## Controlling Licenses

Each repository, component, file, and document is governed by the license expressly published with it.

A general statement that Civizen is open source does not override the actual license attached to a particular work.

Where no license is published, no permission should be inferred beyond rights provided by law.

## Trademarks and Official Identity

Open-source permissions do not automatically grant rights to use:

- the Civizen name,
- the Mature Humanity name,
- logos,
- seals,
- certification marks,
- official domains,
- app-store identities,
- institutional endorsements,
- or statements that a fork, chapter, product, or organization is officially recognized.

Unofficial forks and adaptations must not misrepresent themselves as the official Civizen service.

## Official and Unofficial Versions

The official Civizen service is identified through published domains, repositories, application identities, and institutional records.

Independent versions may be created where the applicable license permits, but must clearly disclose that they are independent and must not create confusion about endorsement, governance, security, or data responsibility.

## Core Institutional Assets

Official brands, domains, institutional records, canonical repositories, public-interest governance materials, and other mission-critical assets must be held or controlled under arrangements that support continuity, accountability, and mission stewardship.

No investor, vendor, administrator, contributor, or service provider obtains ownership merely by funding, hosting, developing, or operating part of the system.

## Mature Humanity Book and Author Materials

The Mature Humanity book, the founder’s personal writings, biography, likeness, and other author materials remain separate intellectual property unless they are expressly assigned or licensed.

Civizen may use such materials only within the scope of the applicable permission or agreement.

## Contributions

Code, content, design, research, translations, and other contributions must have a clear rights basis.

The applicable terms may include:

- an open-source license,
- a contributor agreement,
- a copyright assignment,
- a work-made-for-hire agreement,
- a content license,
- or an institutional partnership agreement.

## Data

Personal information, identity records, private communications, and community data are not treated as unrestricted intellectual property available for commercial exploitation.

Data collection and use must be governed by privacy, security, consent, retention, access, and accountability rules.
