# Transparency and Accountability Standard

## Public Accountability

Civizen will maintain public information sufficient for participants, funders, partners, contributors, and affected communities to understand:

- the initiative’s mission,
- current legal status,
- governing structure,
- material policies,
- major programs,
- funding classifications,
- institutional relationships,
- conflicts of interest,
- material governance changes,
- open-source status,
- and progress toward stated public-interest goals.

## Financial Reporting

Public financial information must be based on authoritative records.

Prototype, simulated, pledged, indicated, pending, and received amounts must be clearly distinguished.

No public dashboard may describe a number as received, spent, audited, distributed, restricted, or available unless the underlying records support that description.

## Annual Accountability Report

When an operating entity exists, the initiative should publish an annual report containing, as applicable:

- governing body and leadership,
- mission and program activity,
- sources and uses of funds,
- major institutional funding,
- material restrictions,
- related-party transactions,
- executive compensation methodology,
- independent review or audit status,
- conflicts and recusals,
- community and contributor participation,
- open-source and technology stewardship,
- privacy and security reporting,
- significant incidents,
- material policy changes,
- and corrections to prior reports.

## Decision Transparency

Material governance decisions should disclose:

- the question decided,
- the responsible decision-maker,
- the source of authority,
- the decision date,
- material conflicts,
- the result,
- and the reason, except where confidentiality is lawfully required.

## Corrections

Material errors must be corrected visibly.

Corrections should identify:

- the original statement or data,
- the corrected information,
- the date of correction,
- and the reason for the correction.

## Protected Information

Transparency does not authorize publication of:

- personal identifying information,
- private donor information where protected,
- banking or wallet information,
- tax records,
- health information,
- private communications,
- credentials,
- security vulnerabilities,
- infrastructure secrets,
- confidential legal advice,
- personnel files,
- protected investigations,
- or information restricted by law or contract.

## Prototype Labels

Any page using demonstration, estimated, simulated, local-only, test, or incomplete data must display that status prominently and must not imitate an audited or official report.
