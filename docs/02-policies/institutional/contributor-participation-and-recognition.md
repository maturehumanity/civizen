# Contributor Participation and Recognition Policy

## Contributor Paths

Civizen may involve:

- volunteers,
- open-source contributors,
- employees,
- independent contractors,
- fellows,
- researchers,
- educators,
- translators,
- moderators,
- advisers,
- institutional partners,
- and community participants.

Each path has different rights, responsibilities, review procedures, intellectual property terms, compensation rules, and legal consequences.

## No Automatic Employment or Compensation

Creating an account, submitting a proposal, contributing code or content, participating in governance, earning a score, receiving recognition, or helping the community does not automatically create:

- employment,
- contractor status,
- wages,
- benefits,
- equity,
- royalties,
- revenue participation,
- reimbursement,
- or a right to payment.

Compensation exists only when established through an approved employment relationship, contract, grant, fellowship, bounty, prize, or other written program.

## Recognition

Civizen may recognize contributions through:

- public attribution,
- contribution records,
- badges,
- credentials,
- acknowledgements,
- maintainer status,
- advisory participation,
- community responsibilities,
- references,
- or other non-financial forms of recognition.

Recognition is not money, property, a security, or a guarantee of future compensation.

## Compensation

Where compensation is offered, the applicable written arrangement must identify:

- the responsible legal party,
- the scope of work,
- deliverables,
- payment terms,
- expenses,
- tax responsibilities,
- confidentiality,
- intellectual property,
- conflicts,
- review and acceptance,
- termination,
- and dispute procedures.

## Contribution Review

Contributions may be evaluated for:

- relevance,
- quality,
- reliability,
- originality,
- evidence,
- safety,
- maintainability,
- mission alignment,
- accessibility,
- and compliance with applicable standards.

A score or review does not create a financial entitlement.

## Intellectual Property

Contributors retain or grant rights only as stated in the applicable repository license, contributor terms, or written agreement.

Contributors must not submit material they do not have the right to contribute.

Official Civizen names, logos, domains, certifications, and institutional endorsements may not be used without authorization.

## Conduct and Conflicts

Contributors must disclose material conflicts and must not use contribution access to:

- manipulate governance,
- conceal sponsorship,
- insert malicious code,
- compromise privacy,
- misrepresent credentials,
- exploit confidential information,
- or obtain improper private benefit.

## Community Participation

Contributors may participate in community and protocol governance according to published eligibility and conflict rules.

Community participation does not replace the legal authority or fiduciary duties of an organization’s governing body.
