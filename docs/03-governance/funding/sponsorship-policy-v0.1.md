# Sponsorship policy v0.1

**Status:** Stub / draft  
**Related:** Funding Constitution §3 (Sponsorships lane)  
**Counsel review:** Not started

## Purpose

Allow mission-aligned sponsorships and partnerships without confusing them with investments or unrestricted donations.

## Rules

1. Sponsorship terms must state deliverables, branding rights, duration, and whether any surplus is unrestricted.
2. Sponsors do not receive investor participation rights unless a separate compliant instrument says so.
3. Mission-alignment and conflict-of-interest review before acceptance.
4. Public disclosure of material sponsorships when transparency policy requires it.

## Prohibitions

- Sponsorships that imply Civizen endorsement of illegal or harmful activity.
- Quiet conversion of sponsorship into equity-like rights.

## Product behavior

- Use institutional inquiry form (`/fund/institutional`) or a future sponsorship lane on interest forms.
- Classify as `sponsorship` when the ledger exists.
