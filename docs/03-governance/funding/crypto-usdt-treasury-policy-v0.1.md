# Crypto and USDT treasury policy v0.1

**Status:** Stub / draft  
**Related:** Funding Constitution §13  
**Counsel review:** Not started  
**Product default:** **USDT / digital asset acceptance is disabled** until this policy and custody are live.

## Purpose

Define how Civizen may accept and hold approved digital assets without creating uncontrolled treasury risk or anonymous capital where identity is required.

## Rules

1. Accept USDT or other assets only through a compliant treasury process.
2. Require KYC/KYB, sanctions screening, source-of-funds review, wallet screening, tx hash, chain ID, and USD fair value at receipt.
3. Maintain separate wallets/accounts by funding lane where practical.
4. Default bias: convert most digital-asset receipts to USD or approved low-risk holdings unless treasury governance approves exposure.

## Prohibitions

- Anonymous capital when identity verification is required.
- Holding excessive digital-asset exposure without approved policy.
- Mixing crypto investor capital with restricted grant wallets.
